Task 3 · The fan-facing agent

Outside the building, the calculus changes.

Same data foundation, different audience. Millions of fans, untrained and anonymous, asking in dozens of languages — and none of them will tolerate a confidently wrong answer.

01

Who you're now building for

Tasks 1 & 2 — Inside the building

A small, known population

  • Trained on the tool. You can teach the limits.
  • Known users. Front office, scouts — a finite list.
  • Workarounds available. For the questions documents can't answer.
Task 3 — Outside the building

Millions, untrained & anonymous

  • Anonymous users. No login, no profile, no history.
  • Dozens of languages. The whole fanbase, not just English.
  • No documentation read. No tolerance for hallucination.
  • No admin chrome. They want a fan experience, not an admin tool.
02

Each difference becomes a requirement

Four shifts in the audience map to four concrete architectural requirements — plus one constant that ties them all together.

The difference What it requires
No admin chrome on a public site
◧ An embeddable widget, not a separate console
Fans ask in dozens of languages
A文 Multilingual support out of the box
A fan audience, not an analyst audience
♥ A friendly, fan-voiced persona — not the scout's analyst voice
Zero tolerance for confidently wrong answers
⊕ A scope tight enough the agent never wanders
Same source of truth across every surface
≡ The same authoritative data foundation behind every answer
