02

This pattern has a name: RAG

Retrieval-Augmented Generation

LLMs reason well over text — but they don't know your documents, and they can't be trusted to invent specifics. RAG fixes that by retrieving the most relevant chunks at query time and giving them to the model as grounding.

The split
The model does the reasoning

It reads the retrieved passages and composes an answer.

 
The documents do the remembering

That's why answers can cite specific sources — the citations are the chunks handed to the model.
